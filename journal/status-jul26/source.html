Sly's Status Update For July 2026

Hello, friends! It's been a while since I've written here, and I've been a bit neglectful of the website in general, so I wanted to type out some ramblings on how the year has gone so far. And what a strange year it's been. 2025 was more hectic for me (moving house, and undergoing excitements and changes at work); and yet I felt more focused, and was able to finish Jade Spring. Now in 2026 I've become physically settled, but mentally (and emotionally) all over the place! But we just celebrated Matariki here in NZ, a mid-year chance to renew and replenish, and so I'm grateful to you all for reading as I ramble and make sense of things for the six months ahead.

Dwelt: Iron Solstice

My second (and original) game Dwelt went on an unintentional hiatus, after I had a bit of a mid-life crisis about it! It's only in the past week or so that I've come back to it feeling more refreshed. The broad conceit has remained relatively unchanged, and I delivered a more formal pitch to friends a few months ago, which went well. My crisis really boiled down to cutting back the scope creep that had accumulated since then, and I'm excited to have a much simpler and clearer picture of the game in my head now (as well as some music I'm happy with). Dwelt updates will get their own journal entry, coming soon!

I'm not very confident that I can have a working demo by the end of the year as I'd originally hoped, but I'm more optimistic that I can keep up a more sustained (if still gentle) pace of game development. I love making games so much; the struggles I feel come from the fears and doubts associated with working on an original game (i.e. not being able to rely on someone else's concepts and mechanics, like in Jade Spring). I wrote in February that I felt "emotionally disconnected from [the game]", and I'm happy and relieved that I don't feel that way anymore.
