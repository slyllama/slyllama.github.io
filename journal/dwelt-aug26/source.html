Dwelt: Development Notes #1

(Heads up: this might be a bit of a long post! I've written about Dwelt in a few places -- such as here and, more recently, here -- but I've never taken the time to lay everything out in preparation for actually making the game. If you're reading this, I really appreciate that you would stop by for a little while and take a look. Thank you so much!)

Up until recently, Dwelt has been more of an umbrella term for a collection of game-centered thoughts, ideas, characters, songs, and sketches I've collected over the years than it has a fully-fledged game. You can see a little of its origins on my old site. During these explorations I was seeking a specific vibe, one encompassing "the memory of the land", and the space between loneliness and solitude.
