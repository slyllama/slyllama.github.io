DRAFT

: Development Notes #1

(Heads up: this might be a bit of a long post! I’ve written about Dwelt in a few places — such as here and, more recently, here — but I’ve never taken the time to lay everything out in preparation for actually making the game. If you’re reading this, I really appreciate that you would stop by for a little while and take a look. Thank you so much!)

Background

Up until recently, Dwelt has been more of an umbrella term for a collection of game-centered thoughts, ideas, characters, songs, and sketches I’ve collected over the years than it has a fully-fledged game. You can see a little of its origins on my old site. During these explorations I was seeking a specific vibe, one encompassing “the memory of the land”, and the space between loneliness and solitude. Dwelt started off being about spirits shepherding the archived souls of the dead, before the concept of robots started to take hold in a more concrete way, and I have done a few design and implementation experiments now!

Robots are powerful vessels for themes of memory, connection, and identity. Mechanical creatures who gain sentience or who process emotions in a different way to humans face unique personal journeys and trials; and our own experiences of the world can be understood through their experiences and struggles.
