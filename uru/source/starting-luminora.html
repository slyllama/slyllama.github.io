Shorah! $EM=smile I've been meaning to get into Age-writing for MOULA like a year now, and at last I'm making some headway! I spent a while going through a bunch of first-time issues caused by my mistakes or things I didn't know, but I've managed to get a good workflow going, especially with the help of very kind people on the Cyan community Discord. I want to document the stuff I've been working on here, and also write down some of those things I worked through. It's a good reminder for me, and it might be useful to someone who stumbles across this page!

The first Age I'm working on is called Luminora at the moment. I have a more ambitious world that I want to make eventually (called the Cradle Age, a big, house-sized steampunk hand sticking out of the ocean), but I should learn the concepts in a much smaller and simpler project first. That project, Luminora, is a greenhouse set just underground which holds a delightful little surprise - but uncovering it will require some puzzle-solving from the player.

The overall layout of the Age is really simple. In plan it's just a circle ringed with columns and arches:
