Dwelt: Iron Solstice is a cozy building and cleansing game, where hope and melancholy co-exist in rich steampunk environments that are yours to explore and heal.

Design, build, cleanse, and garden in Dwelt: Iron Solstice, a steampunk game set in an overgrown post-apocalyptic world, featuring diverse environments to cultivate, and robust construction features. Play as a robotic armature, seeking to heal and rejuvenate a planet abandoned by humanity in the aftermath of a cataclysmic war. Unlock building components by cultivating and gardening each land -- using them to build and decorate your own settlement -- and begin to heal and reconnect a fractured planet.

This page (and the game itself) is in active development -- please check back soon! For now, you can read a little more about the game in this journal entry, and take a look at its origins on my original website.
